Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ lastUpdated }}

This is a personal, experimental project, not a large company with a dedicated privacy team. This page describes, plainly, what data MCP Everything collects and why, so you can decide whether you're comfortable using it.

What we collect

We collect the minimum needed to run your account and the service, which is:

  • Account information: your email address, and a hashed password if you sign up with email rather than GitHub or Google
  • Content you submit to generate servers, such as repository URLs, descriptions, and any conversation you have with the AI to refine a request
  • The MCP servers you generate or host, and basic metadata about them (name, status, timestamps)
  • Basic usage information needed to enforce free-tier generation limits
  • If you sign in with GitHub or Google, the basic profile information those providers share (such as your name, email, and avatar) as part of standard OAuth sign-in

How we use it

Your data is used to operate your account, generate and host the MCP servers you request, enforce usage limits, and keep the service reasonably secure. We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Third parties

Running the service means some of your data passes through a small number of third parties:

  • Anthropic's API, to process the AI requests that analyze your input and generate MCP servers
  • GitHub and Google, if you choose to sign in or sign up through them
  • The infrastructure that hosts this application and any MCP servers you deploy

Each of these handles data under its own terms and privacy practices, which are outside our control.

Data retention

We keep account data and generated content for as long as your account is active, so the service works as expected. If you delete your account, we'll remove what we reasonably can; some records may be retained briefly afterward for operational or security reasons, such as backups aging out.

Your rights

You can review or update your account information from the account page at any time. You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us. If you're located somewhere with specific data protection rights (such as access, correction, or deletion rights under a regional privacy law), those requests are welcome too, contact us and we'll do our best to help.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, such as hashing passwords and requiring authentication for account and generation actions, but no service can guarantee perfect security. Use your own judgment about what you submit.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests about your data, can be sent through the contact details on your account page, or by opening an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
